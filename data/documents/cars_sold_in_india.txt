Cars Sold in India - Overview

India is one of the largest automobile markets in the world. Many
domestic and international car manufacturers sell vehicles in India
across different segments such as hatchbacks, sedans, SUVs, and electric
vehicles.

Car Categories

1.  Hatchbacks
    -   Small and fuel efficient
    -   Example: Swift, Baleno
2.  Sedans
    -   Comfortable family cars
    -   Example: Honda City, Hyundai Verna
3.  SUVs
    -   Higher ground clearance and spacious interiors
    -   Example: Creta, Scorpio, Nexon
4.  Electric Vehicles (EVs)
    -   Battery powered cars
    -   Example: Tata Nexon EV, MG ZS EV

Major Trends in Indian Car Market

-   Growing demand for SUVs
-   Increasing popularity of electric vehicles
-   Focus on fuel efficiency
-   More connected car technology
-   Safety features becoming important

Popular Fuel Types

-   Petrol
-   Diesel
-   CNG
-   Electric

Conclusion

The Indian automobile market continues to grow rapidly with increasing
demand for safe, fuel-efficient, and technology-rich vehicles. SUVs and
electric vehicles are currently among the fastest-growing segments.
